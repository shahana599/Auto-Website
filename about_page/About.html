Our story

We built Auto because
getting help shouldn't be hard

Auto started as a simple idea — a single place to book vehicle services or get emergency help, without the hassle of calling around. We believe that when your car breaks down, your phone should be the solution, not another problem.

How it started

From one frustrating night to a full platform

It started after a late-night breakdown on a highway with no idea who to call. Searching through contacts, calling garages that didn't pick up — that experience became the reason Auto exists.

So nobody else has to go through that same frustration. We partner with verified mechanics and service providers across Kerala to make sure help is always close, always fast, and always trustworthy.
